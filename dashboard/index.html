Is the difference real, or luck?

Difference in RMSE with a 95% confidence interval from a bootstrap over floats. Filled: significant after Holm correction.

Each float reports every ten days along a slowly drifting path, so its measurements are not independent. The tests therefore treat the float as the unit of evidence: per-float average errors go into a paired t-test, and the confidence interval comes from resampling whole floats 2,000 times.

Testing 15 depths at once raises the chance of a false discovery, so every p-value is adjusted with Holm’s method before it is compared with 0.05.

Who is in the data

Raw error would be unfair to the Bay of Bengal: its thermocline is sharper, so every method, even the average, misses by more there. Skill measures each sea against what the average achieves in that same sea.
